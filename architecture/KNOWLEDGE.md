# NOVA-EVO v0.2 — KNOWLEDGE SYSTEM

## 1. Purpose

The Knowledge System is the persistent learning layer of NOVA-EVO.

Its purpose is to transform experience into reusable knowledge.

Knowledge may originate from:

- experiments,
- successful architectures,
- failed architectures,
- debugging,
- repairs,
- resource measurements,
- training results,
- mentor suggestions,
- repeated observations.

The system must distinguish verified knowledge from hypotheses.

## 2. Core Principle

An event is not automatically knowledge.
